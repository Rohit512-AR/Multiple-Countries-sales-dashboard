# 🌍 Multiple Country Sales Dashboard – Power BI

This Power BI project presents a detailed sales performance analysis across multiple countries using a dataset of 1,000,000 transactions from 2020 to 2025. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand revenue trends, product performance, and country-wise contributions, enabling strategic decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard includes the following key visualizations and KPIs:

### 🔹 Sales Trends Over Time
- Line chart showing sales performance across years and months.
- Useful for identifying seasonal trends and year-over-year growth.

### 🔹 Sales & Quantity by Product Category
- Stacked Column chart showing sales and Donut chart showing quantity sold per product.
- Helps assess product demand and pricing strategy.

### 🔹 Monthly Sales Comparison by Country
- Multi-line chart to compare country performance month-by-month.
- Useful for regional strategy planning.

### 🔹 KPIs (Cards)
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- countries Sales

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- **Top Performing Countries**: Identify countries with the highest revenue.
- **Year-over-Year Growth**: Analyze how total sales have changed from 2020 to 2025.
- **Product Strategy**: Understand which product categories drive the most sales.

---

## 📁 Dataset Info

- **Rows**: 1,000,000
- **Fields**: Date, Country, Product, Quantity, Unit Price, Sales Amount
- **Source**: Synthetic data generated for portfolio demonstration
